I am a Ph.D. candidate at the [UNSW NLP group](https://unswnlp.github.io/), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Aditya Joshi](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/aditya-joshi/). My Ph.D. deals with developing language technologies and resources to support linguistically diverse nurse-patient communication and safely support clinical decisions in the emergency department.

I also hold a master's degree from the [University of New South Wales](https://www.unsw.edu.au/), where my thesis dealt with evaluating and improving the robustness of language models to dialects of English. Beyond my research, I mentor undergraduate students through the [Taste of Research](https://www.unsw.edu.au/engineering/student-life/undergraduate-research-opportunities/taste-research) program at UNSW, and manage the [Natural Language Processing course](https://www.unsw.edu.au/course-outlines/course-outline#year=2026&term=Term%201&deliveryMode=In%20Person&deliveryFormat=Standard&teachingPeriod=T1&deliveryLocation=Kensington&courseCode=COMP6713&activityGroupId=1).
