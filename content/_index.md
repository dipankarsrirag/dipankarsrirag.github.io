I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at UNSW Sydney and a member of the [UNSW-NLP group](https://unswnlp.github.io/), supervised by [Dr. Aditya Joshi](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/aditya-joshi/), [Salil Kanhere](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/salil-kanhere) and [Padmanesan Narasimhan](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/padmanesan-narasimhan). My research builds and evaluates language technologies for the emergency department: simulating nurse-patient triage conversations, testing how large language models reason over them, and supporting communication with linguistically diverse patients.

Before my PhD, I studied the robustness of language models to dialects of English, the subject of my master's thesis at UNSW. I teach [natural language processing](https://www.unsw.edu.au/course-outlines/course-outline#year=2026&term=Term%201&deliveryMode=In%20Person&deliveryFormat=Standard&teachingPeriod=T1&deliveryLocation=Kensington&courseCode=COMP6713&activityGroupId=1) at UNSW and mentor undergraduate researchers through the [Taste of Research](https://www.unsw.edu.au/engineering/student-life/undergraduate-research-opportunities/taste-research) program.
