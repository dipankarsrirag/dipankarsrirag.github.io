I am a PhD candidate at the [UNSW-NLP group](https://unswnlp.github.io/), where I am fortunate to be advised by [Dr. Aditya Joshi](https://www.unsw.edu.au/staff/aditya-joshi/). My research deals with developing language technologies and resources for the emergency department: simulating nurse-patient triage conversations, building triage support systems, and aiding communication with linguistically diverse patients.

My master's thesis evaluated and improved how language models understand conversations in different dialects of English. I manage [COMP6713: Natural Language Processing](https://www.unsw.edu.au/course-outlines/course-outline#year=2026&term=Term%201&deliveryMode=In%20Person&deliveryFormat=Standard&teachingPeriod=T1&deliveryLocation=Kensington&courseCode=COMP6713&activityGroupId=1) at UNSW and mentor undergraduate researchers through the [Taste of Research](https://www.unsw.edu.au/engineering/student-life/undergraduate-research-opportunities/taste-research) program.
